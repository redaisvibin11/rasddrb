Overview

Ras ddarb is an amazing inclusive community based in Morocco. We have members with different interests so you always have someone to talk to about anything.

What makes us different

  • We have 3 main languages: Arabic, English, and French, so we can communicate with people from all over the world.
  • We have text chat and voice chat channels so you can converse with fellow members whichever way you find comfortable (chilling, gaming, yapping).
  • We have channels to show off your talents to other members, and we are open to any suggestions for improvement.
  • We play video games especially amoung us with each other from time to time and it is super fun so you can join us and cure your boredom
  • We have a translator to accomidate all our foreigner freinds so they feel welcome in our community

Our level system

🐣

Newbies

Requirements: Join the server you will be at level 0 granting you the role.

Perks: Basic text and voice chats to level up.

🗿

MEMBER

Requirements: Chat and reach level 5.

Perks: Grants you access to the soundboard in voice chat.

😎

RESPECTED

Requirements: Reach level 10.

👴🏽

OG

Requirements: Reach level 20.

Perks: Grants you access to the soundboard & screensharing in voice chat.

👑

VIP

Requirements: Reach level 40.

Perks: Grants you access to soundboard, screensharing & priority voice detection.

note: the server is always being updated so additional perks might be added soon through the server's mods.
